# Ephemeral Capacity & Spot Silicon Hunter (Antigravity Marketplace Plugin)

## Overview
The **Ephemeral Capacity** skill empowers Antigravity agents to discover, evaluate, and rank the lowest-cost cloud spot capacity worldwide across Google Cloud Platform (GCP), AWS, and Azure. 

It indexes underlying **Silicon ChipGrammar architectures** (e.g., Google TPU v8i 4096-bit, AMD EPYC Zen 4 AVX-512, Google Axion ARM SVE2, NVIDIA H100 Hopper) and connects directly to the **Nautilus Three-Border Compiler** to compile customer workloads for the optimal spot node in a single action.

---

## Capabilities & Workflows

### 1. Spot Capacity Candidate Screening
Agents query the **Architecture Hunter** to find the top candidate nodes matching specific compute requirements:
* **Target Silicon:** `google_tpu_v8i`, `amd-epyc-zen4`, `avx512`, `arm64-neoverse-v2`, `nvidia_h100_sxm5`
* **Price / Preemption Filter:** Evaluates hourly spot rates, historical monthly volatility, and preemption risk (`VERY_LOW`, `LOW`, `MEDIUM`, `HIGH`).
* **Validation Output:** Delivers ranked candidate nodes with dry-run commands for subscriber verification.

### 2. "AS-IS" Best-Effort Intelligence Contract
All data is provided on a best-effort historical basis. Subscribers are provided with dry-run commands (`gcloud compute ... --dry-run`) to validate live spot availability and quotas against their cloud credentials.

### 3. The 1-Click Nautilus Compiler Upgrade Funnel
When a customer identifies their target candidate node (e.g. `c3d-standard-4` in `us-east5` or `v8i-standard-4` in `us-east1`):
1. **Identify Architectural Fit:** The skill reports the exact **`ChipGrammarID`** (`CHIP_GRAMMAR_AMD_ZEN4` or `CHIP_GRAMMAR_GOOGLE_TPU_V8I`).
2. **Offer 1-Click Compilation:** If the customer's subscription includes the compiler add-on, the agent immediately invokes `nautilus.exe` to lower and vectorize their workspace.
3. **Seamless Marketplace Expansion:** If the compiler add-on is not active, the customer can toggle the subscription directly in Antigravity Marketplace with zero re-authentication.
